PRIMARY HOMICIDE & CORRUPTION DOSSIER

Case File #DN-402: The Alder Creek Drowning & The Missing 1924 Ledger

Opened: October 20, 2026 • Status: ACTIVE / OBSTRUCTED BY SHERIFF
CRITICAL PRIORITY

Investigation Summary

At 04:15 AM on October 20, the corpse of Arthur Pendelton (Chief Custodian of the Blackwood County Hall of Records) was recovered from the sluice gate of the abandoned Alder Creek Timber Mill. Sheriff Harlan Cross ruled the death an accidental drowning within hours, refusing to order an independent autopsy or secure the crime scene.

Simultaneously, 1924 Municipal Deed Book 8 was illicitly removed from the courthouse sub-vault. That ledger holds the original public easement grant preventing Mayor Thaddeus Vance's syndicate from constructing the $40 million luxury riverfront resort and golf club.

Forensic & Physical Evidence Log

  • Sluice Weir Cable #2: Sheared with industrial hydraulic cable cutters at roughly 01:30 AM.
  • Victim’s Eyeglasses: Wireframe gold spectacles recovered 40 yards upstream in mud with fresh blood smears (Type O-negative, matching Pendelton).
  • Vehicle Imprints: Heavy twin-rear-tire truck impressions matching a 1996 International flatbed, leaving heading toward Old Ironworks.
  • Vault Access Keycard: Sub-vault entry log records Keycard #04 (registered to Sheriff Harlan Cross) swiped at 11:58 PM.

Suspects & Key Figures Ledger

DOSSIER PROFILES

Thaddeus Vance

MAYOR

Age: 54 • In Office: 2018–Present

Primary champion of the $40M Alder Ridge Resort initiative. Vance's private development firm secretly holds options on all parcels adjoining the old mill. If Pendelton's 1924 ledger proved the land was public domain, Vance faced bankruptcy and federal indictment.

STATED ALIBI: Claims he was dining at the Riverfront Club until 11 PM, then asleep at his residence.

Harlan Cross

SHERIFF

Age: 59 • Sheriff: 2012–Present

Hasty to declare the drowning an accident. Ordered the weir basin dredged within six hours, destroying trace tire and boot impressions. Financial records show Cross owes $68,000 in gambling arrears to the Dover Harbor Club, guaranteed by Vance's brother.

STATED ALIBI: Claims he was on lone highway patrol between Mile Marker 12 and 18; radio was switched off.

Helena Sterling

HEIRESS

Age: 42 • Mayoral Candidate

Granddaughter of timber baron C.W. Sterling. Running against Vance on an anti-development platform. However, leaked probate records show her estate receives 12% royalties if the mill boundary is rezoned commercial rather than municipal.

STATED ALIBI: Claims she was at Sterling Manor drafting election flyers; reported a burglary at midnight.
