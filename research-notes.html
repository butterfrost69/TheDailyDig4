EVIDENCE #01 • SCENE OF DEATH FORENSIC

Sheared Sluice Gate Control Cable

The steel cable controlling the timber sluice weir was not worn or rusted through. Clean diagonal shear marks indicate industrial 24-inch hydraulic cutters were used. When the cable snapped, the sluice gate dropped violently, generating a vortex current that pulled Arthur Pendelton under.
GM TRUTH: Julian Finch owns the exact hydraulic cutter model that left the tool marks. His cutter was coated in oil matching the residue on the cable clamp. Finch was ordered by Mayor Vance to "jam the sluice" so the river basin flooded before dawn.

EVIDENCE #02 • SUB-VAULT 3-C THEFT

Missing 1924 Municipal Deed Book 8

Torn leather binding fragments found on the floor of the county sub-vault. The logbook shows Keycard #04 was swiped at 11:58 PM on October 19. Keycard #04 is assigned to Sheriff Harlan Cross. The deed book contained the irrevocable 99-year public trust clause on Alder Ridge.
GM TRUTH: Sheriff Cross didn't swipe the card himself—Mayor Vance took Cross's master keycard from the sheriff's cruiser glovebox while Cross was drinking at the Riverfront Club. The missing deed is currently locked in the trunk of Vance's black sedan!
Inspect Vault Log →
EVIDENCE #03 • CORONER MEMO MEDICAL

Lungs Contained Chlorinated Tap Water

Preliminary fluid sample taken by assistant coroner Miller before Sheriff Cross took the specimen jars: The water in Pendelton's lungs contained chemical chlorine and pipe minerals, not river algae. Pendelton did not drown in the rushing river. He was drowned elsewhere and deposited in the weir.
GM TRUTH: Artie was confronted in the industrial wash basin of the Old Ironworks office (which has municipal tap water) by Julian Finch and Vance. When he refused to surrender his briefcase keys, he was held under the maintenance sink.
Confidential Memo #22
EVIDENCE #04 • WEIR EMBANKMENT TRACE

Riverfront Club Matchbook with Scribbled Code

Found forty feet from the water's edge in deep mud beside Artie's cracked eyeglasses. The matchbook is from the exclusive Riverfront Club (where Mayor Vance is president). Inside the flap, someone penciled: GATE CODE: 7 - 4 - 1 - 9 (STAR).
GM TRUTH: This keypad code unlocks the North Private Gate of Sterling Manor leading to the river boathouse! Helena Sterling gave this code to Mayor Vance during private settlement negotiations three weeks earlier.

EVIDENCE #05 • PROBATE RECORD MOTIVE

Grandfather Codicil: Disputed Parcel 84-C

Helena publicly opposes the resort, yet her grandfather's 1982 will codicil stipulates that if Alder Ridge is rezoned for commercial use, the Sterling Trust automatically receives a 12% perpetual gross royalty on all resort revenues.
GM TRUTH: Helena is playing both sides: if Vance's vote passes, she makes millions; if Vance is disgraced, she wins the mayor's seat. She intentionally left her study safe unlocked so Vance's operative could steal the 1924 survey without implicating her!

EVIDENCE #06 • SIGHTING & TRACE VEHICLE

Twin Rear Tire Track Imprints at Sluice Road

Gravel tire impressions match an International Harvester 4700 dual-axle flatbed. Finch's truck was seen parked outside the scrap yard at 05:00 AM with fresh river reed weeds wrapped around the rear differential and river mud still dripping from the wheel wells.
GM TRUTH: Finch transported Arthur's body in the truck bed hidden under a tarpaulin of scrap iron, dumping him into the weir at 02:15 AM after cutting the sluice wire. Finch can be pressured to confess if players present the tire plaster casts!

EVIDENCE #07 • DIGNAN'S DESK PHONE TIMESTAMPS

Arthur Pendelton's Final Telephone Call

Artie told Dignan Dough: "The 1924 title survey was signed by Mayor Vance's father. It guarantees the entire river basin to the public forever. I photocopied page 44 and hid the duplicate in Safety Deposit Box 14 at Blackwood National Bank."
GM TRUTH: The key to Box 14 was hidden inside the lining of Pendelton's leather hat, which was recovered by the assistant coroner and placed in the personal effects locker at Blackwood Hospital morgue!

EVIDENCE #08 • EMERGENCY WIRE AUDIO

Voice Analysis of the 03:58 AM 911 Caller

Audio recording reveals background jukebox music playing "Blue Valley Waltz", a song only stocked in the jukebox of the River Road Tavern. The caller has a distinct wheezing cough and mentions a municipal Lincoln sedan.
GM TRUTH: The caller was Deputy Ray Harris, the junior night patrol officer. Harris saw Mayor Vance's car parked at the mill, realized something terrible happened, but was too terrified of Sheriff Cross to identify himself. Harris will testify if players offer him protection!

MASTER SOLUTION: WHAT REALLY HAPPENED ON OCTOBER 19-20?

The Conspiracy: Mayor Thaddeus Vance, faced with personal bankruptcy, orchestrated the $40M Alder Ridge Resort plan. Arthur Pendelton discovered that Vance's development company had forged the land title by deleting the 1924 municipal easement.

The Execution: Vance paid Julian Finch $25,000 to steal the original ledger from the vault. Using Sheriff Harlan Cross's keycard (which Vance swiped from Cross's cruiser while Cross was drinking), Finch took the ledger. When Pendelton confronted them at the Old Ironworks, Finch drowned Artie in the wash sink. They transported Artie to the mill, sheared the weir cable to simulate an accidental fall, and dumped him in the river.

The Victory Condition for Players: Players win by retrieving the duplicate deed page from Safety Deposit Box 14 (or recovering the original from Vance's Lincoln trunk) and delivering it to Dignan Dough or the District Attorney before the town council vote on Thursday at 7:00 PM!
